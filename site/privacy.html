Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 16, 2026

1. Overview

Mystical is a desktop app that helps you manage PC game libraries from multiple platforms. The app is designed to keep your data on your own device.

2. Data Stored Locally

Mystical stores data locally on your device, including settings, cached metadata, and cover art files. This data is used only to provide app features.

3. Account Connections and Platform Data

If you connect services such as Steam or Epic, Mystical reads available local launcher data and optional API responses required to show your library. Mystical does not run its own backend service for account data.

4. Network Requests

The app may make network requests to third-party services for game metadata, deal listings, and cover art (for example Steam, Epic, GOG, Xbox, Microsoft Store, and IGDB when configured).

5. Data Sharing

Mystical does not sell your personal data. Data is not shared by Mystical to third parties except through direct requests made from your device to services you choose to connect.

6. Third-Party Terms

When you use connected platforms, their privacy policies and terms also apply.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the project repository.
